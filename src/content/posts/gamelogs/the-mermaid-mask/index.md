I liked this one more than Tangle Tower. It is a definite evolution on the previous game, and hope to see more like this one day. In fact, all deduction games should learn from this one.

I played The Mermaid Mask with both my partner and my kids, as we are all fans of deduction mystery games. Althoug the focus was solving the murder, the style and subject matter surrouding the murder was only spooky rather than scary. Plus-- being the follow-up to [Tangle Tower][1] which we also all played through together meant this game was going to get played by all of us one way or another.

## What I Didn't Like

### Leaps in Deductive Logic

I feel that this isn't a problem with _this specific game_ but rather with the genre of deductive reasoning games.

There were a few times that we were very confident in our answers only to find out that we were wrong, and there was another leap in logic we needed to make.

This sort of thing is common in deduction games. In fact, I would probably argue that is meant to be part of the experience. But, it's just not my thing. I laugh it off and it doesn't take away from the experience for me, but I find it a little frustrating.
