Video games are software, but they're not like any other software.

I have been dealing in software development for almost two decades at this point, but the more I tackle creating a commercial video game, the more I see how game development is just not the same.

My journey in game development continued with another completed iteration where game design was the focus. As the game started taking shape and a project started to form, the idea of _scalable game design_ entered my mind.

Is scalable game design a thing?

This is what I reflect on in the devlog for this iteration.
